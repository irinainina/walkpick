Health

What a walk does for you

  • Mood

    You set out in one mood. You come back in a better one.

  • Weight

    Kilos leave quietly, step by step. Every walk counts, even a short one.

  • Heart and blood pressure

    Your heart likes it when you move. Your blood pressure does too.

  • Glucose

    On a walk, glucose gets used instead of building up in your blood.

Check it on your own chart.

If you find it useful, log your mood, weight, blood pressure or glucose, and see how regular walks relate to these numbers.

WalkPick is a diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat.
